1Who We Are

EX-Ray (“EX-Ray”, “the app”, “we”, “us”) is operated by M2Code, based in Serbia. EX-Ray helps you understand relationship dynamics by using artificial intelligence to analyze conversation screenshots and the context you provide. This policy applies to the EX-Ray mobile application on iOS and Android.

2Information We Collect

We only collect data that is necessary to operate the app:

  • Account & identity. If you sign in with Apple or Google, we receive your name and email address (Apple may provide a private relay email). If you continue as a Guest, we generate an anonymous local ID — no sign-in or personal identity is collected.
  • Relationship context. Information you voluntarily enter: your first name, the other person’s name, genders, relationship status, duration, and the situation or concern you describe.
  • Conversation screenshots. Images you upload for analysis, which may contain messages, names, and other content involving you and other people.
  • Analysis history. The AI-generated reports we create and store so you can revisit them.
  • Subscription data. Your subscription status, plan, and purchase history, provided to us by RevenueCat and the app stores. We never receive or store your full payment-card details.
  • Technical data. Your chosen app language, device/OS type, and basic diagnostic information used to keep the app stable.

3How We Use Your Data

  • To generate your AI analysis and the recommended next steps.
  • To save and display your personal analysis history.
  • To verify and manage your subscription and unlock entitlements.
  • To maintain, secure, debug, and improve the app.
  • To respond to your support requests.
  • To comply with legal obligations.

4How the AI Analysis Works

When you request an analysis, your screenshots and the context you entered are transmitted over an encrypted connection (TLS) to the Google Gemini API, which processes them in real time and returns a structured psychological-style analysis. We use your content only to generate your result — we do not use it to train our own models, and we do not build advertising profiles from it.

5Third-Party Services

We rely on a small number of trusted providers (“sub-processors”) to run EX-Ray. Each receives only the data needed for its function:

  • Google Gemini — AI processing of your screenshots and context.
  • Supabase — secure cloud database, authentication, and storage for your account and history.
  • RevenueCat — subscription management and entitlement verification.
  • Apple & Google — sign-in and payment processing through the App Store and Google Play.

These providers maintain their own privacy and security practices and process data under their respective terms.

6Screenshots & Other People

Conversation screenshots may contain personal information about other people. You are responsible for ensuring you have the legal right or consent to upload and analyze any content you submit. Please do not upload material you are not permitted to share. We treat all uploaded content as confidential and use it solely to provide your analysis.

7Data Retention

We keep your account data and analysis history for as long as your account is active. Uploaded screenshot images are transmitted for processing and are not retained as part of your permanent profile once the analysis is generated. When you delete your account or request deletion, your associated data is permanently removed from our servers within 30 days.

8How We Share Data

We share data only with the sub-processors listed in Section 5, strictly to operate the app, and where required by law. We do not sell your personal data and we do not share it with third-party advertisers.

9Your Privacy Rights

Depending on where you live (including under the GDPR and CCPA), you may have the right to access, correct, delete, or export your data, to withdraw consent, and to object to or restrict certain processing. To exercise these rights, use the in-app tools or contact us (Section 15). You also have the right to lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority.

10Account & Data Deletion

Delete your data You can delete your account directly in the Profile section of the app. Alternatively, email markoandrijevic27@gmail.com with the subject “Account Deletion Request.” All associated data — account info, analysis history, and any references to uploaded images — is permanently deleted within 30 days.

11Data Security

We use industry-standard encryption to protect your data in transit (TLS/SSL) and at rest, along with access controls limiting who can reach it. No method of electronic transmission or storage is 100% secure, but we work continuously to safeguard your information.

12Children’s Privacy

EX-Ray is intended for adults and is not directed to anyone under the age of 17 (or the minimum age of digital consent in your country). We do not knowingly collect data from children. If you believe a minor has provided us data, contact us and we will remove it.

13International Transfers

Our providers may process and store data on servers located outside your country, including in the United States and the European Union. Where required, we rely on appropriate safeguards to protect your information during such transfers.

14Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. When we do, we will revise the “Last updated” date above, and we will notify you of material changes within the app. Continued use of EX-Ray after an update means you accept the revised policy.
